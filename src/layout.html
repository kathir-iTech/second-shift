Review a priority change

  1. Inspect a missed ticket. The archive opens on incidents that would disappear from the review if you used their latest fields.
  2. Save your expectation. Choose the priority this ticket should receive, add a reason, and save it as a test case.
  3. Change the policy. Use “Edit this policy cell” to change the proposed priority. Check whether your saved case now passes.

For your own data, open the app inside Jira and choose Jira project. All priority edits are simulations.
